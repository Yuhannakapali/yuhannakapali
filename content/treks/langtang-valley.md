Langtang is the closest of the big valleys to Kathmandu, and for years it was the one I put off. I regret that now. You can be on the trail the same morning you leave the city, and by the second day the forest opens into a glacial valley with Langtang Lirung standing over all of it.

## The route in short

Most people start at Syabrubesi and walk up through Lama Hotel to Langtang village, then on to Kyanjin Gompa. From there the day hikes are the reward: Kyanjin Ri for the sunrise, Tserko Ri if your legs and lungs agree.

## Before you go

Carry cash, respect the acclimatisation days, and eat at the village lodges. The valley rebuilt itself with very little outside help, and the tea houses are the economy. Spend there.
