Welcome to my blog. This site has no server and no database. Every post is a plain markdown file that lives in the repository, and the site is rebuilt and redeployed automatically whenever a new file lands on the main branch.

## Why a git-based blog

Keeping posts in git means the writing is versioned like code. There is nothing to back up, nothing to pay for, and no admin panel that can go down. I can edit a post from my laptop, from the CMS at `/admin`, or from the built-in editor at `/write`.

## What comes next

More posts on backend engineering, the systems I build, and the occasional note on tooling. Thanks for reading.
